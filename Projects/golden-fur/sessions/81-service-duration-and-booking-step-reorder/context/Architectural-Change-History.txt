Part 1 — Issues Raised During the Session
The advisor (Ms. Mayuga) walked through a live demo of the Golden Fur booking system. The issues below are organized by area, as surfaced during that walkthrough.
A. Payment & Compliance
1. PayMongo requires business verification (KYC).
When the team attempted to log into PayMongo with the Golden Fur account, the platform required ID/business verification (Know Your Customer) before payment processing could be enabled, since PayMongo is a licensed payment portal. This requirement was not identified prior to the capstone proposal being finalized, so it wasn't accounted for in the original scope.
2. Facebook Login requires Meta Business Verification.
Similarly, enabling Facebook Login (Meta for Developers) requires Meta Business Verification once the app is live to the public. This is a second compliance dependency the team was not previously aware of.
3. Down payment was configured per-service instead of per-transaction.
During testing, the down payment toggle was found to apply at the individual service level rather than across the entire transaction. Per the proposal's scope, the down payment requirement should apply to all online payments/bookings as a whole.
B. Booking Flow
4. Reschedule allows selecting a date before the current booking's day.
While testing reschedule, a 'previous day' option remained selectable even though the booking had already been moved to a later date. Once a booking is moved forward, earlier dates (already past) should no longer be selectable.
5. Multi-day (3-day) booking configuration may not be working consistently.
A minimum-3-days filter/configuration was tested (e.g., Sept 1–3), but the advisor and dev noted it might not be functioning properly, and that schedules appeared to be 'pinned' or locked in an unexpected way.
6. A newly created service type did not appear in the booking flow.
A new 'Massage' service was created and marked available for both branches, but it did not show up in the 'Book a Service' step/menu during the customer booking flow.
7. The system only supports booking one pet at a time.
Customers with multiple pets cannot book services for more than one pet within the same session (e.g., one pet for Makati, another for Southwoods, or two pets at the same branch). For hotel-type bookings specifically, only one cage can be assigned per booking, so multiple pets cannot each be assigned their own cage in a single transaction. The advisor acknowledged this is a nontrivial database/architecture challenge, not just a UI fix.
C. Services & Packages
8. Running total miscalculates when an individual service overlaps with a package.
When a service that also exists inside a selected package was chosen individually, the displayed price did not update correctly (e.g., showed 750 instead of the expected 630) until the conflicting selection was manually removed.
9. Unclear selection order and labeling between individual services and packages.
When a service is already part of a selected package, it becomes disabled — but it isn't clearly labeled with which package it belongs to, making the code/reasoning hard to read. The advisor also raised the open question of whether individual services or packages should be the default/first thing a customer selects.
D. Cancellations & Credits
10. Cancellation always converts payment to a full (100%) credit, with no configurable option.
The system currently performs a full conversion of payment to customer credit upon cancellation. The advisor suggested this should be configurable (e.g., an admin-defined percentage, such as 50%, returned as credit instead of the full amount).
11. Credits were not generated correctly during a cancel/reschedule test.
While testing the cancel-then-reschedule path, no credit appeared to have been generated from the cancelled/down-paid booking, which should have converted per policy.
E. Environment & Deployment
12. The system is still running on localhost, limiting real-world testing.
The advisor pointed out that because the system has not yet been deployed to a live environment, certain payment- and integration-related behaviors (e.g., PayMongo, OAuth logins) cannot be fully verified end-to-end.

Part 1 — Issues Raised During the Session (Addendum)
These issues were surfaced in the same advisory walkthrough but were left out of the first document. They are organized by area.
A. Down Payment & Slot Reservation (flagged as critical)
1. Online bookings can currently reserve a slot with zero payment.
The advisor was explicit that an online booking must never be allowed to hold a slot without some form of payment — either full payment or a down payment. Without this, a customer could reserve a slot and simply not show up, wasting it.
2. Down payment amount needs to be configurable two ways: percentage or flat fee.
The owner (Sir Mike) may want a straight percentage (e.g., 50%) or a fixed flat amount (e.g., ₱200) regardless of the total service cost. The system should let admins configure either mode.
3. An unpaid down payment should NOT lock the booking into the queue — the slot must stay bookable by others.
This is the core issue: a booking with an unpaid down payment should sit in a 'Pending' / pencil-booking state and the time slot should remain visible and bookable by other customers, not treated as reserved/confirmed. Only an actual payment (full or down) should move it into a real, locked slot.
4. Unpaid down-payment reservations need an expiration window.
The advisor described a same-day deadline (e.g., pay by 11:59 PM) after which the reservation auto-resets and the slot becomes available again the next day. There is currently no such timeout/expiration mechanism.
5. No 'advance payment' option — book online now, pay in person on the day.
The advisor asked whether a customer could book online but only pay when they physically arrive for the service. This should be supported as a distinct 'advance payment' path, with the cashier able to manually mark it as paid on arrival.
B. Cancellation Flow
6. Cancellation currently executes immediately with no confirmation prompt.
During testing, clicking Cancel went through right away (with only a reason text box), when it should show a proper 'Are you sure you want to cancel?' confirmation step first.
7. Cancellation screen doesn't disclose the non-refundable/credit-conversion policy to the customer.
The advisor dictated specific messaging that should appear on-screen: payment (full or down) will not be refunded, but will be converted into account credit usable on the customer's next visit. This messaging currently isn't shown anywhere in the flow.
C. Notifications & Reporting
8. Email booking-confirmation notifications are not yet implemented.
A booking was confirmed in-app, but no confirmation email was sent, since the email API integration hasn't been wired in yet — only the in-browser notification currently works.
9. The Daily Sales Report (DSR) module does not exist yet.
When asked about reporting, the team confirmed there is no report/DSR functionality at all yet — a gap against the proposal's stated objectives.
D. Booking Status Logic
10. The system currently assumes a booking is paid/started even without a real payment module.
Because there's no live payment module yet, the flow was found to treat a booking as already paid/in-progress by default, rather than accurately reflecting that no payment has actually been confirmed.
E. Payment Provider Scope (Defense Contingency)
11. PayMongo integration may not be ready in time for the defense — a fallback plan is needed.
Since PayMongo's business verification is still pending owner sign-off, the advisor suggested using a placeholder 'payment type' selector (no live processing) for the thesis defense, with real PayMongo setup happening after deployment and before turnover, once the owner approves. This needs to be explicitly written into the paper's scope section, not just handled in code.
F. Third-Party Login
12. The advisor recommended removing Facebook Login outright, not just deferring it.
Since Google OAuth and email/password login are already implemented and sufficient, the advisor's guidance was to drop Facebook Login from scope entirely rather than pursue Meta Business Verification.
G. Website Content & Verification Coordination
13. Site images are AI-generated placeholders pending professional photos.
The team is still waiting on professional photos from the business; current images are AI-generated stand-ins. Separately, some business-verification information cannot be provided by Sir Mike alone and needs coordination with the COO (the owner's spouse).
H. Deployment / Environment
14. The site is deployed, but the latest local changes have not been merged/pushed yet.
This means recently discussed fixes aren't live yet, and environment-only bugs (that don't show up on localhost) can't be caught until a merge and redeploy happen.
I. Packages & Pricing Clarity
15. Package discount percentage isn't clearly communicated to the customer.
In the example discussed, a package dropped a price from 700 to 630 (a 10% discount), but this percentage/savings isn't clearly surfaced in the UI — customers just see the final bundled price.
J. Receptionist, Assessment & Walk-in Workflow
16. There is no proper walk-in booking flow yet.
Walk-in registration is expected to happen on the day/time of service itself (not as a future appointment), but this distinct flow hasn't been built — the system currently only supports the same appointment-style flow for everyone.
17. Pet assessment (weight, coat type) must be recorded on-site by the receptionist and directly affects pricing.
A pet that hasn't been assessed yet has no weight or coat-type data, which blocks accurate pricing for grooming/hotel/daycare services until the receptionist records this in person.
18. 'Miscellaneous' service category is confusingly named and should be 'Assessment Types.'
MISC is currently used as the category for pets that haven't been assessed yet, but the label is too broad/confusing (it could imply misc. services or payments) and should be renamed for clarity.
19. Bug: the Assessment/MISC category does not appear in service type search results.
This blocks the assessment step entirely for unassessed pets, since staff can't find/select the assessment service type through search.
K. Cage Assignment
20. Customers can currently pick their own cage during booking — this should be receptionist-only.
The advisor was clear that cage selection should not be a customer-facing choice; it should be assigned by the receptionist/staff instead.
21. Cage assignment should be for monitoring only and should not affect pricing.
The advisor confirmed cage size/selection has no bearing on the price — it exists purely so staff can track which pet is in which cage.
L. Multi-Pet Booking
22. The system still only supports one pet per booking, with no interim solution implemented.
This was raised again in more concrete terms: rather than true simultaneous multi-pet selection, an interim option discussed was allowing the same customer to book multiple pets on the same day at staggered times, working around limited staff availability.

Add an expiration timer to unpaid down-payment reservations (e.g., due by end of day); auto-release the held slot if the deadline passes unpaid.
